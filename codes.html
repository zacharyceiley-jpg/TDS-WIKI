Codes

Codes are little gifts that the developers sometimes give out that can be redeemed for Experience EXP, Coins, Gems, or sometimes towers, skins and emotes. Some codes are given after purchasing a certain item, with each one being unique.

You can redeem codes by inputting a code into the code box, which is found on the top right of your screen in the form of an icon with the letter "ABX" whenever you are in the lobby.

For skin codes, you need to have the tower in question to redeem the code. If you do not have the tower, you cannot redeem the code.

Merchandise Codes

As of 18th of May 2025, these codes are no longer given out. Instead, the items are now obtained through the in-game Merch Shop.

These codes were randomly generated upon the purchase of the product in question. They were unique and could only be redeemed once for one person. These codes did not expire until they were redeemed.

If the product associated with that code was refunded, the reward was revoked and removed. For unredeemed codes, if the product was refunded, the code was voided.
