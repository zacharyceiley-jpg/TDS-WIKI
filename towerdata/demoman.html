Description

The Demoman is a starter splash damage tower that can be bought in the store for 200 Coins. While targeting a specific enemy, it also affects nearby enemies, dealing splash damage.

At Levels 0–2, the Demoman takes one second to aim at an enemy before throwing its explosive grenades. The explosion has no damage drop-off, and the Demoman can damage Lead enemies.

The Demoman is effective against large hordes of non-boss enemies, but is relatively weak against major bosses. It is considered an early-game to mid-game tower, with late-game use for clearing swarms.
