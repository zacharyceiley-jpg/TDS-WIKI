Executioner

Executioner is an offensive ground tower unlocked by triumphing Totality Tower. It throws a sharp axe that ricochets between enemies, gaining added damage with each contact. It is strongest against groups, but its damage output drops significantly against a lone enemy. It has flying detection from Level 0 and hidden detection from Level 3.

Strategy

Executioner is strongest when its axe can bounce between groups of enemies. Its damage bonus increases with each bounce, so it can deal much less damage when only one enemy is in range. Place it where enemies bunch up, and upgrade to Level 3 if hidden detection is needed.
